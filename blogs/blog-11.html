The Rise of Pet-Inclusive Travel

India’s travel landscape has transformed dramatically over the last decade. From quick weekend escapes to long road trips and luxury stays, the idea of “family” now organically includes fur babies. According to leading travel agencies, searches for pet-friendly hotels and resorts in India have more than doubled in recent years.

The shift stems from a lifestyle evolution; young urban couples and families, especially in metros like Mumbai, Pune and Bengaluru, are redefining what quality time means. For many, it’s not just about exploring new destinations but doing it with their loyal companions, whether that’s a sprightly Beagle sniffing his way through Coorg’s coffee trails or a gentle Golden Retriever paddling through the calm backwaters of Kerala.

The Emotional Connect: Pets as Family

In Indian homes, the emotional status of pets has changed. A Labrador or an Indie rescue is no longer the “family dog”; they’re “the baby” or “the sibling”. With this shift comes an emotional responsibility: leaving them behind in kennels often feels like abandoning a family member.

Take Delhi-based marketing consultant Anisha and her husband Karan. Their Cocker Spaniel, Pogo, travels everywhere they go. From pet-friendly cafes in Bhimtal to road trips to Rishikesh, they’ve made travel adjustments around their pet’s comfort, selecting stays with open lawns, pet menus and even grooming tie-ups. “We realised Pogo enjoys the hills more than any of us”, laughs Anisha. “He’s part of every plan; our vacations feel fuller with him around”.

The trend symbolises a generational mindset, emotional inclusivity over convenience. For today’s Indian families, the joy of shared experiences trumps the effort of accommodation.

Travel Brands Are Catching Up

The hospitality and tourism sector in India has quickly caught on to this demand. Big names like Taj, Hilton and Marriott now list pet-friendly properties in their portfolios, offering specialized pet suites, curated meals, and even “pet concierges”.

In boutique and homestay ecosystems, the change is even more pronounced. From Goa’s beachside bungalows with “pet zones” to Himachal’s cozy chalets welcoming Huskies in winter, small hospitality owners are realising that pets bring occupancy, loyalty and a community of like-minded guests.

Platforms like Airbnb and MakeMyTrip now offer filters for “pet-friendly stays”, helping families narrow down ideal destinations. Meanwhile, even transport providers; from Zoomcar to Blusmart, are promoting flexible, pet-accommodating journeys.

Favourite Destinations for Pet Parents

Across the Indian map, a few destinations are emerging as hotspots for pet-inclusive holidays:

  • Lonavala and Pawna Lake (Maharashtra): Perfect for short weekend breaks from Mumbai or Pune, these regions offer lawns and open spaces where dogs can roam leash-free.
  • Coorg (Karnataka): The cooler climate is ideal for breeds like Labradors, Shih Tzus and Indies who enjoy hiking trails and outdoor experiences.
  • Ooty and Coonoor (Tamil Nadu): Beautiful weather for long walks and pet-friendly heritage stays.
  • Goa: Beach bliss for retrievers, Indies and Spaniels, many beach shacks now have “dog menus” with boiled chicken, fish and rice bowls.
  • Manali and Tirthan Valley (Himachal Pradesh): Perfect for bigger breeds like Huskies or German Shepherds, especially during snow season.

Each of these destinations has evolved to serve a specific type of traveller, one who arrives with their pet’s travel bag packed with treats, water bowls and a favourite blanket.

Road Trips: The New Pet-Friendly Movement

One of the biggest enablers of pet-friendly vacations in India is the growth of road trip culture. With reliable highways and improved property listings, more families now prefer to drive; giving pets the comfort of space, frequent breaks and familiar scents.

SUVs and hatchbacks alike are being modified with pet seat covers, car harnesses and portable crates. For breeds that love movement: think Beagles, Boxers or even Indies who’ve known street freedom, the open road is the ultimate thrill.

What’s more, cafes, fuel stations and eateries along major travel routes are learning to accommodate traveling pets, offering water bowls and shaded rest spots. It’s an ecosystem reshaping Indian travel, one pawprint at a time.

The Breeds and Their Travel Quirks

While destination selection is often emotional, many families also plan trips based on their pet’s breed and temperament.

  • Golden Retrievers and Labradors love open water and outdoor terrains, making beaches, lakesides, and hill stations ideal.
  • Shih Tzus, Pugs and Toy Breeds prefer controlled climates with shaded areas and shorter walks, ideal for cozy stays or heritage properties in cooler regions.
  • Indie dogs, the unsung heroes of resilience, adapt to most weather, thriving in both hill towns and sea breeze getaways.
  • Huskies have sparked unique winter tourism, with families heading to snow-laden regions like Manali or Gulmarg to let them experience their “natural habitat”.

This subtle alignment of breed behaviour and travel preferences showcases the maturity of Indian pet parenting, it’s not just emotional; it’s deeply thoughtful.

Travel Planning Essentials for Pet Parents

Pet-friendly travel is wonderful but it demands foresight. A few essentials can make the experience seamless:

  1. Check accommodation policies. Confirm size/breed restrictions, additional fees and available pet-friendly zones.
  2. Pack familiar items. Carry their regular food, medicines and a comfort toy to reduce travel anxiety.
  3. Prioritise safety. Use car seat belts or travel crates and avoid overfeeding before long drives.
  4. Maintain hydration. Indian weather can be unpredictable; portable water bowls are a must.
  5. Be courteous travellers. Keep leashes handy, clean up after pets and avoid disturbing other guests.

When done right, pet-inclusive trips can be both liberating and deeply emotional. You don’t just travel, you create shared memories that your pet feels, smells and remembers.

The Future of Pet Tourism in India

What’s happening now is the start of a much larger movement. India is poised to see specialised pet resorts, pet travel agencies and even pet adventure camps in the coming years. With social media amplifying every adorable moment, travel with pets has also become aspirational, part lifestyle statement, part heartfelt joy.

Local communities and brands are recognising this shift, from dog-friendly breweries in Bengaluru to luxury trains considering future pet compartments. Soon, “leave them with the neighbour” might truly become a thing of the past.

In the End, It’s About Togetherness

At the heart of this new wave of Indian travel lies one simple truth, love is best experienced together. For every Retriever chasing waves in Goa or every Indie curling up by a Shimla fireplace, there’s an Indian family rediscovering connection, warmth and pure joy.

Because when you travel with your pet, you don’t just change your routine; you expand your sense of home. The destination becomes secondary; the companionship becomes the journey itself.
