Pet Hosts vs Pet Hotels: What Today’s Indian Pet Parents Really Prefer

By PetBnB Team • Updated December 2025

In today’s fast-evolving India, pets are no longer just animals who share our homes. They’re integral members of the family: companions, confidants and emotional anchors. As urban lifestyles grow busier, families are travelling more, working longer and relocating frequently. This has made one question more important than ever: who looks after our beloved pets when we can’t?

Over the past few years, the Indian pet care industry has witnessed a quiet but significant shift. Pet hotels once ruled the space, offering structured boarding facilities and standardised care. But a new wave is changing the scene: the rise of pet hosts: everyday people who open their homes and hearts to care for pets like their own. And increasingly, Indian pet parents are choosing them over traditional pet hotels.

Understanding the difference

Before exploring the preference, it’s important to understand what differentiates a pet host from a pet hotel. Pet hotels work much like human hotels; rooms, check-ins and sometimes, even premium suites. They’re designed to provide safety, meals and scheduled playtime. Most are professional establishments with trained staff, groomers and veterinarians on call.

Pet hosts, on the other hand, offer something far more personal. They’re individuals or families who invite pets into a home setting, recreating the comfort, attention and affection pets receive from their own parents. Instead of kennels or rooms, pets share living spaces, couches, and sometimes, beds. For animals sensitive to loud sounds or separation anxiety, this can feel like being home away from home.

The emotional side of pet care

Indian pet owners are deeply emotional about their animals. For many, leaving a pet behind, even for a few days, comes with guilt and apprehension. Stories of pets returning withdrawn after a stay in a commercial boarding facility are not uncommon. That’s where pet hosts strike a chord. Their homely environment and one-on-one care reconnect with what pet owners instinctively want: emotional security for their pets.

When pets live under the same roof as their host, they receive affection beyond feeding schedules. They roam freely, interact naturally and are spoken to rather than handled like clients. This emotional continuity is something no standardised facility can replicate, no matter how well equipped.

A reflection of changing lifestyles

The preference for pet hosts also mirrors the changing lifestyles of India’s urban population. Millennials and Gen Z pet parents approach animal care differently. They prioritise emotional wellbeing over convenience, quality over quantity and trust over transaction. Many grew up watching their parents treat pets functionally but their own values stem from empathy and companionship.

For them, leaving a dog or cat at a facility with cages and timers feels outdated. They’d rather choose a host they can chat with directly, check in through video calls and receive updates about how their companion is settling in. It’s less about outsourcing care and more about extending their pet’s social circle with someone who genuinely cares.

Comfort over convenience

Pet hotels market their services through convenience and reliability. There’s a clear structure — drop-off timings, curated meals, set recreation hours. Yet, what they often lack is flexibility. If your pet wants a late breakfast, doesn’t enjoy being around eight other dogs or prefers to sleep at the foot of a bed, a hotel can’t accommodate those nuances.

Hosts, on the other hand, adapt. They often have pets of their own, understand these individual quirks and adjust routines accordingly. They walk when needed, cuddle when the animal seeks affection and create a space where pets feel seen, not managed. For anxious or senior pets, this difference alone is transformative.

The economics of trust

While trust is not easily quantifiable, it does hold economic weight. Pet hotels usually charge premium rates to cover staff, facilities and overheads. Pet hosting offers a more balanced model, often more affordable, yet deeply personalised. Indian pet parents see value in that human connection: knowing that there’s a real person sending updates, not a front desk using a templated schedule.

Moreover, hosts typically take on fewer pets at a time, focusing on individual attention rather than volume. This smaller, curated approach enhances reliability and accountability. The relationship feels more human, less transactional: a key differentiator in a market built on trust.

The social element: community and belonging

Another reason pet hosting resonates in India is the social aspect. Our culture values warmth, relationships and connection. The idea of entrusting one’s pet to a like-minded person within the same city or neighbourhood feels natural. Many pet hosts start as neighbours or acquaintances and end up forming lasting friendships with the pet parents they serve.

This sense of community-driven care aligns with Indian values. It revives a spirit of interdependence, reminiscent of how families once helped each other with children or household support. In many ways, pet hosting taps into that collective ethos, only now, it’s facilitated through trusted networks and technology.

Pet wellbeing at the centre

Pet wellbeing today goes far beyond meals and medical checks. It includes mental stimulation, emotional balance and social connection. In large boarding facilities, even with the best intentions, pets sometimes experience stress. Strange environments, multiple smells, and unfamiliar handlers can trigger anxiety, especially for sensitive breeds.

Hosts create a calmer alternative. Their smaller home settings reduce sensory overload and allow consistent human presence. Dogs still go for walks, eat balanced meals and play with familiar toys but they also receive something irreplaceable: touch, talk and time. These are simple yet powerful reassurances that help maintain emotional stability during separation.

Technology and transparency

Technology has played an important role in the rise of pet hosts. Verified profiles, ratings and reviews build transparency that earlier only corporate-run facilities could offer. Pet parents can browse hosts, read testimonials and communicate directly before making a decision. Add real-time updates, photos and videos and hosts now offer a visibility far beyond traditional boarding houses.

This transparency has reassured Indian customers who value both safety and accountability. Combined with growing awareness about pet psychology and behavioural needs, the digital transformation of pet care has accelerated the move toward host-based models.

Beyond pet care: the human connection

One of the most underrated benefits of pet hosting is the emotional satisfaction it provides both sides. Hosts often speak of how fulfilling it feels to care for another’s pet, while pet parents express gratitude for the bond their animals form with these caregivers. It’s more than service, it’s companionship extended through trust.

In a world increasingly defined by convenience-driven transactions, this rekindles something genuine: human connection. And that ultimately benefits the pet most of all.

The verdict: heart over habit

India’s pet parents are clearer than ever about what matters most. While pet hotels remain an option for some, the tide is firmly turning toward pet hosting. It fits seamlessly into a culture that values relationships, responsiveness and emotional authenticity.

Ultimately, what pets need mirrors what humans crave: comfort, familiarity and love. Pet hosts offer all three, without the formality of structured systems or sterile spaces. For modern Indian families seeking care that feels human at heart, hosts represent not just an alternative but a better, more compassionate way forward.
